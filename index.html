Before You Visit

Questions you might have.

A few things people often ask before their first visit to Solace.

What massage is best if I just want to relax?

Swedish massage is a gentle, full-body treatment designed to encourage relaxation and help you unwind.

Do I need to know which massage I want before booking?

Not at all. Tell us how you're feeling and what you'd like from your visit, and we'll help you choose a treatment that suits you.

How long does a massage take?

Most Solace treatments are available in 60- or 90-minute sessions.

What should I expect during my first visit?

We'll take a few moments to understand what you need before your treatment begins. Your comfort comes first, and you can let your therapist know if anything needs adjusting.

Can I request a female or male therapist?

Yes. You can let us know your preference when booking, and we'll do our best to accommodate your request.

Do I need to book in advance?

We recommend booking ahead so we can reserve a time that works for you. Availability may vary depending on the day.

Can I book a massage for someone else?

Absolutely. A Solace treatment can be a thoughtful gift for someone who deserves a little time to themselves.
